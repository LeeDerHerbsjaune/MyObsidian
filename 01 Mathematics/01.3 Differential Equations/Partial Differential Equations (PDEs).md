# Partial Differential Equations

> [!abstract] Definition
> A Partial Differential Equation (PDE) is a differential equation involving an unknown function of multiple independent variables and its partial derivatives.
>
> PDEs describe relationships between a function, its independent variables, and its rates of change with respect to those variables.

## 1. Mathematical Representation

> [!abstract] General Form
> A PDE of order $n$ can be represented implicitly as:
>
> $$F(\mathbf{x},u,\partial u,\partial^2u,\ldots,\partial^n u)=0$$
>
> where:
> - $\mathbf{x}=(x_1,x_2,\ldots,x_m)$ represents the independent variables.
> - $u=u(x_1,x_2,\ldots,x_m)$ is the unknown dependent function.
> - $\partial^k u$ represents the partial derivatives of order $k$.
> - $n$ is the highest partial derivative order.

## 8. Knowledge Relationships

### Foundation Knowledge

- **Calculus:** Provides differentiation and integration.
- **Multivariable Calculus:** Provides partial differentiation and multiple integration.
- **Linear Algebra:** Supports the study of linear operators and systems.
- **Real Analysis:** Establishes rigorous foundations for convergence and continuity.
- **Measure Theory:** Provides foundations for modern integration and function spaces.

### Downstream Knowledge

- **Functional Analysis:** Provides the abstract framework for studying PDE operators and solution spaces.
- **Distribution Theory:** Generalizes derivatives and supports weak formulations.
- **Sobolev Spaces:** Provide function spaces for weak solutions.
- **Elliptic PDE Theory:** Develops the analysis of elliptic equations.
- **Parabolic PDE Theory:** Develops the analysis of parabolic equations.
- **Hyperbolic PDE Theory:** Develops the analysis of hyperbolic equations.
- **Numerical Methods for PDEs:** Develops computational approximations of PDE solutions.
- **Mathematical Physics:** Uses PDEs to formulate physical laws.
