> [!abstract] Core Takeaway
> Number systems provide the numerical foundation of mathematics. Starting from natural numbers, successive extensions introduce negative numbers, fractions, irrational quantities, and complex values. These systems provide the mathematical domains required for algebra, geometry, calculus, linear algebra, numerical computation, and robotics.
