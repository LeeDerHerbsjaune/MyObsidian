# Applications of Integration

> [!abstract] Definition
> Applications of Integration studies mathematical problems involving accumulation, continuous quantities, geometric measurements, and total changes.
>
> Definite integrals provide a general mathematical framework for calculating accumulated quantities over continuous domains.

## 1. Area Between Curves

> [!definition] Definition
> Integration calculates the area enclosed between two curves over a specified interval.

If $f(x)\geq g(x)$:

$$
A=\int_a^b[f(x)-g(x)]\,dx
$$

## 2. Volumes of Solids

> [!definition] Definition
> Integration calculates the volume of three-dimensional solids by accumulating cross-sectional areas.

### Disk Method

$$
V=\pi\int_a^b[R(x)]^2\,dx
$$

### Washer Method

$$
V=\pi\int_a^b
\left[R(x)^2-r(x)^2\right]dx
$$

### Cylindrical Shell Method

$$
V=2\pi\int_a^b
(\text{radius})(\text{height})\,dx
$$

## 3. Arc Length

> [!definition] Definition
> Integration calculates the length of a curve over a specified interval.

For $y=f(x)$:

$$
L=\int_a^b
\sqrt{1+[f'(x)]^2}\,dx
$$

## 4. Surface Area of Revolution

> [!definition] Definition
> Integration determines the surface area generated by rotating a curve around an axis.

For rotation around the $x$-axis:

$$
S=2\pi\int_a^b
f(x)\sqrt{1+[f'(x)]^2}\,dx
$$

assuming $f(x)\geq0$.

## 5. Physical Applications

> [!info] Work
> For a variable force acting along a one-dimensional path:

$$
W=\int_a^b F(x)\,dx
$$

> [!info] Displacement
> If velocity is a function of time:

$$
\Delta s=\int_{t_1}^{t_2}v(t)\,dt
$$

> [!info] Total Distance
> Total distance traveled is:

$$
D=\int_{t_1}^{t_2}|v(t)|\,dt
$$

## 6. Mass and Center of Mass

> [!info] Variable Linear Density
> For a one-dimensional object with density $\rho(x)$:

$$
m=\int_a^b\rho(x)\,dx
$$

Its center of mass is:

$$
\bar{x}
=
\frac{1}{m}
\int_a^b x\rho(x)\,dx
$$

## 7. Average Value of a Function

> [!definition] Definition
> The average value of an integrable function over an interval is:

$$
f_{\mathrm{avg}}
=
\frac{1}{b-a}
\int_a^b f(x)\,dx
$$

## 8. Probability Applications

> [!info] Continuous Probability Density
> For a probability density function $f(x)$:

$$
P(a\leq X\leq b)
=
\int_a^b f(x)\,dx
$$

The total probability over the distribution's domain equals one.

## 9. Knowledge Relationships

### Foundation Knowledge
- **Definite Integrals:** Provides the mathematical operation for accumulation.
- **Integration Techniques:** Provides methods for evaluating integrals.
- **Functions:** Describes the quantities being accumulated.
- **Fundamental Theorem of Calculus:** Connects accumulation with antiderivatives.

### Downstream Knowledge
- **Multivariable Calculus:** Extends integration to multidimensional domains.
- **Vector Calculus:** Develops line, surface, and volume integration.
- **Probability Theory:** Uses integration to calculate probabilities and expectations.
- **Mechanics:** Uses integration to calculate work, displacement, and mass properties.
- **Numerical Analysis:** Develops numerical methods for approximating integrals.
