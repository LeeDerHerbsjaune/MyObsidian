> [!abstract] Definition
> **Exterior algebra** is a graded algebra constructed from a vector space in which multiplication is alternating. It provides the algebraic foundation for differential forms.
>
> For a vector space $V$, its exterior algebra is
>
> $$\Lambda(V)=\bigoplus_{k=0}^{n}\Lambda^k(V)$$
>
> where $n=\dim V$ and $\Lambda^k(V)$ is the space of alternating $k$-vectors.

> [!important] Construction
> The exterior algebra is generated by the tensor algebra after imposing the relations
>
> $$v\wedge v=0,\qquad v\in V$$
>
> Equivalently, the wedge product satisfies
>
> $$u\wedge v=-v\wedge u$$
>
> for vectors $u,v\in V$.

> [!note] Grading
> The exterior algebra is graded by degree:
>
> $$\Lambda^k(V)\wedge\Lambda^\ell(V)
> \subseteq\Lambda^{k+\ell}(V)$$
>
> If $k+\ell>n$, the resulting space is zero.
>
> Its dimension is
>
> $$\dim\Lambda^k(V)=\binom{n}{k}$$

> [!note] Basis
> Given a basis $\{e_1,\ldots,e_n\}$ of $V$, a basis of $\Lambda^k(V)$ consists of
>
> $$e_{i_1}\wedge\cdots\wedge e_{i_k},
> \qquad i_1<\cdots<i_k$$
>
> The ordering condition avoids redundant elements caused by antisymmetry.

> [!note] Dual Exterior Algebra
> Differential forms are elements of the exterior algebra of the dual space:
>
> $$\Lambda(V^*)=\bigoplus_{k=0}^{n}\Lambda^k(V^*)$$
>
> Its elements are alternating multilinear maps on vectors.

> [!important] Algebraic Properties
> The wedge product is:
> - Bilinear.
> - Associative.
> - Graded-commutative:
>
> $$\alpha\wedge\beta=(-1)^{k\ell}\beta\wedge\alpha$$
>
> where $\alpha$ has degree $k$ and $\beta$ has degree $\ell$.

> [!warning] Scope
> Exterior algebra is an algebraic construction on vector spaces. Differential forms extend this construction smoothly over a manifold by using the cotangent spaces at each point.
