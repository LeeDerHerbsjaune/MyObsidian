### 5.3 Important Distinctions

> [!important] Euclidean space vs. related mathematical structures
>
> | Concept | Main structure | What it provides |
> |---|---|---|
> | Vector space | Addition and scalar multiplication | Linear operations |
> | Inner product space | Vector space + inner product | Angles and lengths |
> | Normed vector space | Vector space + norm | Vector magnitudes |
> | Metric space | Set + distance function | Generalized distances |
> | Euclidean space | Finite-dimensional real affine space + Euclidean inner product | Classical Euclidean geometry |
>
> Every Euclidean space has an associated metric, but not every metric space is Euclidean.
