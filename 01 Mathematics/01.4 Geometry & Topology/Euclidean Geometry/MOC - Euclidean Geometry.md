### 5.3 Comparison with Other Geometries

> [!important] Euclidean vs. non-Euclidean geometry
>
> | Property | Euclidean | Hyperbolic | Spherical |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | Curvature | Zero | Negative | Positive |
> | Parallel lines through an external point | Exactly one | More than one | None in the standard model |
> | Triangle angle sum | \(180^\circ\) | Less than \(180^\circ\) | Greater than \(180^\circ\) |
> | Standard model | Euclidean plane | Hyperbolic plane | Sphere surface |
>
> The table refers to the standard two-dimensional geometries. Spherical geometry is intrinsically curved and should not be confused with ordinary three-dimensional Euclidean geometry containing a sphere.
