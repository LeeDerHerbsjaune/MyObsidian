> [!abstract] Core Takeaway
> Geometric objects are the fundamental entities used to describe spatial structures and configurations. They can be represented through sets of points, coordinate equations, parametric mappings, and geometric constraints. Their mathematical representation provides the foundation for geometric modeling, spatial reasoning, and robotics.
