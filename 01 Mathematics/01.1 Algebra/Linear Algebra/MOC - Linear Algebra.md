# Linear Algebra

> [!abstract] Definition
> Linear Algebra is a branch of mathematics that studies vector spaces, linear transformations, and the algebraic structures associated with them.
>
> Its central objects include vectors, matrices, linear maps, determinants, eigenvalues, and inner products.
>
> Linear Algebra provides a unified mathematical framework for describing and analyzing linear relationships, transformations, and systems of equations.

> [!target] Knowledge Scope
> This knowledge domain covers:
> - Vector spaces and their algebraic properties.
> - Subspaces, bases, and dimensions.
> - Matrices and matrix operations.
> - Linear transformations and their representations.
> - [[Determinants and Invertibility|Determinants and invertibility.]]
> - Eigenvalues, eigenvectors, and diagonalization.
> - Inner product spaces and orthogonality.
> - [[Canonical Matrix Decompositions|Canonical matrix decompositions.]]
